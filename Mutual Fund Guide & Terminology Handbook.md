# Comprehensive Mutual Fund Guide & Terminology Handbook

This handbook is designed to help you understand every aspect of mutual fund investing, read your portfolio dashboard on mobile apps, evaluate performance, and confidently conduct discussions with your financial advisor.

---

## 1. Core Basics & Fund Structure

### Mutual Fund
* **Definition:** A pooled financial vehicle where money from multiple investors is combined to purchase a diversified portfolio of stocks, bonds, or other securities managed by a professional fund manager.
* **Example:** 10,000 investors contribute ₹1,000 each into a ₹1 Crore pool. The fund manager allocates that ₹1 Crore across 40 different companies (e.g., Reliance, TCS, HDFC Bank).
* **Where You See It:** Displayed as the **Scheme Name** on mobile apps (e.g., *Parag Parikh Flexi Cap Fund*, *SBI Bluechip Fund*).

### Asset Management Company (AMC) / Fund House
* **Definition:** The corporate entity registered with market regulators that creates, operates, and manages mutual fund schemes.
* **Example:** SBI Mutual Fund, HDFC AMC, ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund, Axis Mutual Fund.
* **Where You See It:** Filterable under "Fund Houses" on investment platforms or listed at the top of a fund details screen.

### NAV (Net Asset Value)
* **Definition:** The price per single unit of a mutual fund scheme. It reflects the total market value of all holdings minus liabilities, divided by the number of outstanding units, updated daily after market close.
$$\text{NAV} = \frac{\text{Total Market Value of Assets} - \text{Liabilities}}{\text{Total Outstanding Units}}$$
* **Example:** If a fund holds ₹100 Crore in assets and has issued 1 Crore units, its NAV is **₹100**. Investing ₹10,000 gets you **100 units**.
* **Where You See It:** 
  * **Mobile App:** Displayed prominently at the top of a scheme's page (e.g., `NAV: ₹124.50`).
  * **Financial Outlets:** Listed alongside daily performance updates (e.g., `NAV ₹124.50 (+0.85%)`).

### AUM (Assets Under Management)
* **Definition:** The aggregate market value of all investor assets managed by a specific mutual fund scheme or an entire AMC.
* **Example:** An AUM of **₹35,000 Crore** means the fund holds ₹35,000 Crore worth of underlying assets contributed by all unit holders combined.
* **Where You See It:** Listed under **Fund Overview / Details** (e.g., `AUM: ₹32,450 Cr`). A larger AUM indicates high institutional trust and liquidity.

## 2. Market Cap Classifications & Fund Categories

### Equity Funds
Funds investing primarily in equity stocks of companies, aimed at capital growth with higher market volatility.

* **Large-Cap Funds:** Invest in top 100 well-established companies by market capitalization. Offer stability and steady long-term growth.
* **Mid-Cap Funds:** Invest in mid-sized companies ranked 101 to 250. Offer high growth potential with moderate to high volatility.
* **Small-Cap Funds:** Invest in companies beyond the top 250 rank. High growth potential accompanied by sharp short-term price fluctuations.
* **Flexi-Cap / Multi-Cap Funds:** Invest across large, mid, and small-cap stocks. Flexi-cap managers can dynamically alter cap weights based on market trends.

### Debt Funds
Funds investing in fixed-income instruments (e.g., Government Bonds, Corporate Bonds, Treasury Bills) focusing on capital preservation and regular yield.

### Hybrid / Balanced Funds
Funds distributing capital across both equity and debt instruments to strike a balance between appreciation and stability.

### Index / Passive Funds
Funds engineered to track and mirror the composition and performance of a specific index (e.g., Nifty 50, S&P 500) rather than actively picking individual stocks.

### Active Funds
Funds where a professional manager selects specific securities to outperform a benchmark index.

## 3. Investment Modes & Plan Variants

### Regular Plan vs. Direct Plan
* **Regular Plan:** Purchased through an intermediary or distributor. The AMC pays an ongoing commission to the distributor out of your investment, raising the Expense Ratio.
* **Direct Plan:** Purchased directly from the AMC or via direct investment platforms. No distributor commission is charged, yielding **1%–2.5% higher annual returns**.
* **Example:** Over 20 years, a ₹10,000/month SIP in a **Direct Plan** yielding 15% could grow to **₹1.22 Crore**, whereas a **Regular Plan** yielding 13.5% (due to 1.5% advisor commission) grows to **₹1.01 Crore**—a difference of **₹21 Lakhs**.
* **Where You See It:** In the title of the scheme on apps:
  * *HDFC Top 100 - Direct Plan - Growth* vs. *HDFC Top 100 - Regular Plan - Growth*.

### Growth Option vs. IDCW (Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal)
* **Growth:** All profits generated by the scheme are retained and reinvested, compounding over time.
* **IDCW:** Profits are periodically distributed as payouts, reducing the NAV by the exact payout amount.
* **Where You See It:** Filter toggle on scheme pages (`Growth` vs. `IDCW`). **Growth** is generally preferable for wealth accumulation.

### Investment Channels
* **SIP (Systematic Investment Plan):** Automated recurring investment (e.g., ₹5,000 on the 5th of every month).
* **Lumpsum:** Single bulk investment made at one time (e.g., ₹2,000,000 from a bonus).
* **STP (Systematic Transfer Plan):** Periodically transferring a set amount from one fund (e.g., Debt) to another (e.g., Equity).
* **SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan):** Periodically redeeming a fixed sum from a fund, common for generating post-retirement income.
* **Rupee Cost Averaging:** The mechanism in SIPs where fixed monthly investments automatically buy more units when prices fall and fewer when prices rise, averaging down unit costs over time.

## 4. Expenses, Fees & Portfolio Structure

### Expense Ratio (TER - Total Expense Ratio)
* **Definition:** The annual fee charged by the AMC to cover management, administrative, custody, and marketing costs, expressed as a percentage of total assets.
* **Example:** On a ₹1,000,000 portfolio in a fund with a **0.8% TER**, the AMC deducts **₹8,000 per year** (calculated and deducted daily from the NAV).
* **Where You See It:** Under **Fund Info / Charges** (e.g., `Expense Ratio: 0.65%`).

### Exit Load
* **Definition:** A fee or penalty levied by the fund house if you redeem or switch units before a specified period.
* **Example:** *"1% exit load if redeemed within 1 year."* Withdrawing ₹100,000 after 6 months incurs a ₹1,000 penalty, returning net ₹99,000. Withdrawing after 12 months incurs zero exit load.
* **Where You See It:** Under **Scheme Information** or on the order redemption summary screen.

### Portfolio Holdings & Sector Allocations
* **Definition:** The list of specific stocks/bonds owned by the fund and how the capital is allocated across different industry sectors.
* **Where You See It:** The **"Holdings"** tab on mobile apps, displaying top stocks (e.g., Infosys 8%, ICICI Bank 7%), asset split (Equity 95%, Cash 5%), and market cap exposure.

## 5. Reading Mobile App Dashboards & Stock Boards

### Mobile App Portfolio Dashboard Terminology
When opening apps like Zerodha Coin, Groww, Kuvera, or PayTM Money, you encounter these metrics:

| Dashboard Metric | Description | Concrete Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Invested Value** | Net cumulative cash deposited from your bank account. | ₹5,00,000 |
| **Current Value** | Value of held units evaluated against today's closing NAV. | ₹6,40,000 |
| **Total Returns / Gain** | Absolute capital gain/loss (`Current Value - Invested Value`). | **+₹1,40,000 (+28%)** |
| **1-Day Return** | Portfolio gain or loss relative to yesterday's closing price. | **+₹3,200 (+0.5%)** |
| **XIRR** | Extended Internal Rate of Return; accurate annualized return for recurring cash flows (SIPs). | **15.4%** |
| **Folio Number** | Unique account ID assigned by the AMC for holding unit records. | `Folio: 91028374/12` |

### Stock Boards (NSE / BSE) vs. Mutual Fund NAVs
* **Stock Board Tickers (Live):** Prices of listed stocks and indices update second-by-second during trading hours (e.g., **NIFTY 50** `24,850.30  +120.40 (+0.49%)`).
* **NAV Processing:** Mutual Fund NAVs **do not change second-by-second**. They are updated **once per day** (typically by 11:00 PM IST) based on the closing market values of all underlying securities.

## 6. Performance Tracking & Risk Metrics

### Benchmark Index
* **Definition:** A standard stock market index (e.g., Nifty 50, BSE 500, S&P 500) used as a baseline to evaluate a mutual fund manager's performance.

### Performance Indicators
* **CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate):** The annualized growth rate of an investment held for more than one year under compounding assumptions.
* **XIRR (Extended Internal Rate of Return):** Used to calculate true annualized returns for non-lump sum investments like SIPs or periodic withdrawals.

### Risk & Risk-Adjusted Return Metrics
* **Alpha ($\alpha$):** Measures the excess return generated by the fund relative to its benchmark index.
  * *Example:* $\alpha = +3.5$ indicates the fund outperformed its benchmark by 3.5% annually.
* **Beta ($\beta$):** Measures the fund's volatility relative to the benchmark index.
  * $\beta = 1.0$: Volatility matches the index.
  * $\beta = 1.2$: 20% more volatile than the index.
  * $\beta = 0.8$: 20% less volatile than the index.
* **Standard Deviation:** Measures total volatility or price dispersion. Higher values indicate wider return swings.
* **Sharpe Ratio:** Measures excess return earned per unit of total risk taken. Higher Sharpe ratios indicate better risk efficiency.
* **Sortino Ratio:** Similar to Sharpe, but focuses exclusively on downside volatility rather than total volatility.

## 7. Actionable Checklist for Advisor Discussions

Before finalizing any portfolio decisions with your advisor, address these core points:

1. **Plan Type Audit:** *"Are you recommending Direct Plans or Regular Plans? If Regular Plans, what is your ongoing commission rate?"*
2. **Expense Assessment:** *"What is the Total Expense Ratio (TER) of each proposed fund scheme, and are there any Exit Loads or lock-in periods?"*
3. **Benchmark Outperformance:** *"What benchmark index is each fund measured against, and what has its Alpha ($\alpha$) been over 3-year and 5-year horizons?"*
4. **Risk Alignment:** *"How do the Beta ($\beta$), Sharpe Ratio, and cap distributions of this portfolio match my overall risk tolerance and investment horizon?"*
